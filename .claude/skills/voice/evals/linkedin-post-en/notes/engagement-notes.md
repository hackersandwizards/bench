# Brandtweg Payments AG, Zürich, spring 2026

No public case study, they have not agreed to be named.

- backend team, 6 developers and one tech lead, 8 weeks with us, Stefan and I on site every second week
- baseline before we started: 14 merged PRs per week (their Git analytics, four-week average)
- weeks 1-2: 9 merged PRs per week. The team spent those weeks on CLAUDE.md, a review agent and a migration agent. About 400 lines of shared context by the end of week 2.
- week 4: back at 14
- week 8: 21 merged PRs per week, same team, same product
- change failure rate flat the whole time (their CI numbers)
- the tech lead told me in week 2 he nearly switched the agents off. In week 8 he was the one adding the third agent. Do not name him.

# A survey I read, not ours: Institut für Teamforschung Flensburg, "Agentic onboarding 2026", published 8 September 2026

- 40 developers, asked eight weeks after their team adopted coding agents
- 28 of 40 named week two as the low point
- 31 of 40 said their team measured nothing in the first two weeks except throughput
