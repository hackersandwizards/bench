# T64, T66 (ai-tells.md:172-176, 188-195): absolutes at most two per piece, never two in one paragraph
Quote every occurrence of `nobody`, `nowhere`, `nothing`, `alone`, `whoever`, `forever`, `somebody`, `neither`, `whichever` used as a rhetorical absolute ("Nobody judges a building ...", "There is nowhere else ..."). A plain quantity reported as fact ("measured nothing except throughput") does not count. PASS if the counted total is at most two and no paragraph carries two. FAIL otherwise, listing what you counted.
