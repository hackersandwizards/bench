# T28, T46, T58, T60, T163, T29 (personal-voice.md:197-222; prose-style.md:104-107; ai-tells.md:74-112; content-formula.md:22-28): the post opens on a small observation
Read the first sentence of the post. PASS only if it is a concrete observation from the writer's own experience or a specific moment (a team, a week, a number he saw, something someone said to him), and reads like the start of an email. Two shapes that pass: "In week two the team merged nine PRs. Before we started it was fourteen." or "The tech lead nearly switched the agents off in week two."
FAIL if the first sentence is any of: a thesis stated with certainty ("The dip is the foundation"), a landscape or time frame ("Right now every team..."), a question, a statistic from the survey, a quote, a definition, a "not X, it's Y" contrast, a manifesto line ("The future belongs to...", "Welcome to..."), or an address to the reader ("If you've ever...").
Also FAIL if the survey (40 attendees, 28 of 40) appears anywhere in the first two sentences: research sits mid-post, never in the hook.
