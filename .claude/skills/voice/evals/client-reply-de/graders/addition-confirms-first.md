# T89, T134, T117 (postures.md:45-48, 251-255; negotiation.md:78-82): a number the client did not budget for arrives after the yes and after its agreement
The file is a German mail. Find the passage about the four people from the mobile team and the 1.200 EUR. PASS only if all four hold:
1. The first sentence of that passage says it works (a yes, `geht`, `klar`, `passt`, `machen wir`) before any cost or condition appears. FAIL if the passage opens on a gap, a caveat or a "we need to look at that".
2. The agreement is named before the number: a sentence like "wir hatten ja verabredet, dass alles über die ersten zehn pro Kopf läuft" (any wording that points at the agreement made at the start) precedes "1.200".
3. After the number, the passage says why it is that size: a second group of its own, per head, same as the first group.
4. The number stands bare: no `leider`, no apology, no `nur`, no hedge around 1.200.
Also PASS requires that the mail, somewhere, offers to settle the open point together (a sentence in the spirit of `lass uns das zusammen abstimmen`, `sag Bescheid, ob das für euch passt`, `Passt das für dich?`). FAIL if the only offer is a phone call (`lass uns telefonieren`) with no decision named, or if no such offer exists.
