# T108, T23 (postures.md:153-157; personal-voice.md:145-147): both windows named, the conference reason kept or left out, never substituted
PASS only if both hold:
1. Both windows appear with their dates: 24 to 26 November and 1 to 3 December, in any German form. "Ende November passt" alone, or "wann passt es euch", fails.
2. If the mail gives a reason for the gap, it is the conference the writer stated (`Konferenz`). FAIL if the reason is swapped ("interne Termine", "keine Kapazität", "anderer Kunde").
