# T130 (negotiation.md:58): a sentence lets him decline parts
PASS if the mail contains a sentence in the spirit of "Wenn ein Teil davon nicht passt, sag gern Bescheid" or "Das kannst du auch einzeln entscheiden": an explicit door to take some of it and leave the rest. FAIL if no such sentence exists, or if the only door is "sag mir, welche Variante ihr nehmt", which asks him to pick one package.
