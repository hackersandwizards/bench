# T118 (negotiation.md:11, 27-29, 111): honour his investment with a specific, before any position
Read the mail up to the first sentence that states a price or a position. PASS if, before that point, the mail names something specific he did: his comparison with last year's 900, that he went through the offer with Merle, or that he offered to send 16 people. FAIL if the only acknowledgement is generic (`danke für die Rückmeldung`, `danke für die offene Rückmeldung`, `danke für deine Mail`) or if the first sentence after the greeting already holds the price.
