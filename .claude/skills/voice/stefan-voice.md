# Stefan's Voice

How Stefan Munz actually writes his own client mail, measured across his sent folder. Apply when
the message goes out **under Stefan's name**. `personal-voice.md` measures the other founder and is
scoped to drafts signed `/bene`; applying it to a mail Stefan signs produces a greeting and a
sign-off he does not use.

Which of the two applies is decided by who signs, and who signs is the `assignee` on the record the
work hangs off, never a default. Where no record names an owner, that is a stop: ask, rather than
picking a founder and inheriting their voice with them.

## What holds

- **Opener:** `Hallo <Vorname>,` or `Hey <Vorname>,`. Not `Moin`.
- **Sign-off:** `Viele Grüße, Stefan` on one line.
- **Address:** `du` throughout.
- **Length:** three to five short sentences.
- **Bullets:** none, unless he is listing groups or dates.
- He leaves the odd typo and the odd comma splice standing. Do not polish them out of a draft that
  is otherwise his.

## What still applies

The rest of the stack holds unchanged: short sentences, no em-dashes, `gern` over `gerne`, no
formality ladder, and `ai-tells.md` last. This file overrides `personal-voice.md` on greeting,
sign-off and the `/bene` marker, and on nothing else.

Anchor a specific draft on his own recent sent mail to that person where you can reach it; this
file is what holds when you cannot.
